# How to Import Pinterest Boards Directly into Figma for Moodboards & UI Inspiration

For product designers, brand strategists, art directors, and UI/UX professionals, **Pinterest** is the ultimate discovery engine. It's where visual research begins: color palettes, typography experiments, mobile interaction patterns, branding guidelines, and aesthetic moodboards all live on Pinterest boards.

Meanwhile, **Figma** is where design execution happens.

The traditional bridge between Pinterest and Figma, however, is remarkably clumsy:
1. You find a great Pinterest board with 60 pins.
2. You save each image one by one to your computer’s hard drive.
3. You drag-and-drop dozens of files onto your Figma canvas.
4. Your canvas becomes an unorganized heap of overlapping images, and your computer storage is filled with temporary downloads you'll delete tomorrow.

## Step-by-Step Tutorial: Bringing Pinterest into Figma

Here is the exact workflow design teams use to build clean, client-ready moodboards in minutes:

### Step 1: Install the PinGrabber Extension
1. Install [PinGrabber on the Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/pingrabber-download-pinte/gjcmidpgmdnmeggbihgnpkenmnphendk).
2. Pin the extension icon to your browser bar for instant access.

### Step 2: Open and Scroll Through Your Inspiration Board
1. Navigate to your selected Pinterest board (e.g., *Fintech UI Patterns*, *Minimalist Editorial Layouts*, or *E-commerce Branding*).
2. Scroll through the board so that all desired pins render in the viewport.

### Step 3: Copy All Image URLs with One Click
1. Click the **PinGrabber icon** in your browser toolbar.
2. The extension will display the count of detected pins.
3. Click the **Copy URLs** button. All original high-resolution image links are now stored on your clipboard.

### Step 4: Paste into Figma Using the PinGrabber Companion Plugin
1. Open your Figma project canvas or FigJam file.
2. Launch the PinGrabber companion plugin (or compatible image grid importer).
3. Paste your copied URLs into the input box and click **Import to Canvas**.
4. Watch as all 50+ images populate onto your canvas in an organized grid layout, neatly framed and ready for your team review!

*(Alternatively, if you prefer local offline archives, simply click **Download ZIP** inside PinGrabber to save all full-resolution photos to your hard drive in one click).*
