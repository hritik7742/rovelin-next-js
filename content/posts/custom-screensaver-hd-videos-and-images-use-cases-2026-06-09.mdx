## How to Maximize Custom Screensaver’s Potential

To get the most out of your screensaver experience, focus on three key areas: **content quality**, **user behavior**, and **technical optimization**.

### 1. Content Quality
- **Resolution**: Always use HD images (1920x1080 or higher) and videos (1080p minimum).
- **Format**: MP4 for videos and JPEG/PNG for images ensure broad compatibility.
- **Length**: Keep videos under 2 minutes to avoid repetition fatigue.

### 2. User Behavior
Monitor your idle time thresholds carefully:
- **Less than 5 minutes**: Ideal for quick breaks during focused work.
- **5–10 minutes**: Suitable for office environments with moderate movement.
- **Greater than 10 minutes**: Recommended for retail or waiting areas.

### 3. Technical Optimization
- **Bandwidth**: Use compressed video files to prevent buffering.
- **Privacy**: Avoid sensitive or confidential content in public-facing screensavers.
- **Power Settings**: Ensure your system stays awake long enough to display media effectively.

## FAQ: Common Questions About Custom Screensavers

### **How do I install Custom Screensaver - HD Videos & Images?**
   - Visit the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/jkegjaomefbejnfmahjfjidfelncgpma) and click “Add to Chrome.” Follow the prompts to install and configure the extension.

### **Can I use copyrighted videos or images?**
   - Only use media you own or have explicit permission to display. For best results, opt for royalty-free sources like Unsplash, Pexels, or YouTube Public Domain.

### **Will this screensaver work on non-Chrome browsers?**
   - The extension is designed for Chrome. For Firefox or Edge, consider using browser-specific tools or third-party apps like ScreenSaver Plus.

### **What file formats are supported?**
   - Supported video formats include MP4, AVI (with limitations), and WebM. Images should be JPEG, PNG, or GIF.

### **Can I schedule the screensaver to activate only during specific hours?**
   - While the base extension doesn’t support scheduling, you can use automation tools like AutoHotkey or Task Scheduler (Windows) to trigger the screensaver at set times.
