# How to Create a Distraction-Free YouTube Experience with Filtered YouTube [2026 Guide]

YouTube is a double-edged sword for productivity. While it offers an incredible library of educational and professional development content, its interface is designed to keep you scrolling. From the ever-present **recommendations** to the addictive **Shorts** feed, even a quick 5-minute video can spiral into an hour-long time sink. For students, remote workers, and anyone tackling focused tasks, this can be a major roadblock.

Enter **[Filtered YouTube - Remove YouTube Recommended & Shorts](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/bkdalkbneidlmnkafimplljdajddmogb)**. ## The Problem with YouTube’s Default Interface

Before diving into solutions, let’s unpack the design choices that make YouTube so hard to escape. The platform’s layout is engineered to maximize engagement, not productivity. Here’s a breakdown of the main culprits:

### The Recommended Videos Section
The “Up Next” and “Suggestions” sections are designed to feed you content based on your viewing habits. While convenient, this often leads to the “rabbit hole” effect — you start watching a tutorial on Excel shortcuts and end up 45 minutes into a conspiracy theory video.

### YouTube Shorts
Shorts are the TikTok-style vertical videos that can’t be ignored. They autoplay aggressively and are positioned where your eyes naturally drift. Even if you’re watching a long-form educational video, the Shorts feed remains visible in the sidebar or bottom panel.

### Comment Sections
While comments can add value, they’re also a breeding ground for distractions. Endless scrolling through replies, memes, and tangential debates can derail your focus faster than you think.

### Other Clutter
YouTube’s interface includes ads, suggested playlists, and subscription alerts that collectively create a chaotic environment. For tasks like studying, writing, or deep work, this noise is a productivity killer.

## How Filtered YouTube Solves These Issues

**Filtered YouTube** isn’t just a **youtube tool** — it’s a productivity ally. By customizing what appears on your screen, it eliminates the psychological triggers that lead to distraction. Here’s how it works:

### ✅ Removes Recommended Videos
The extension automatically hides the “Up Next” and “Suggested Videos” sections. No more accidental clicks leading to unrelated content.

### ✅ Filters Out YouTube Shorts
The Shorts feed is completely removed, ensuring your view is limited to the content you intentionally choose.

### ✅ Hides Comments and Other Clutter
Optional settings let you hide the comment section, suggested playlists, and even the YouTube search bar — creating a minimalist canvas for focused watching.

### ✅ Customizable Interface
You decide what stays and what goes. Whether you want to preserve the sidebar for later viewing or strip the interface down to just the video player, Filtered YouTube adapts to your workflow.

## How to Install and Configure Filtered YouTube

Setting up the extension is quick and straightforward:

1. **Install the Extension**
   - Visit the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/bkdalkbneidlmnkafimplljdajddmogb) and click **“Add to Chrome.”**
2. **Enable Necessary Permissions**
   - The extension will request permission to modify YouTube’s interface. Confirm to proceed.
3. **Open YouTube in a New Tab**
   - Launch any YouTube video or playlist. Filtered YouTube will automatically apply your saved settings.
4. **Access the Extension Settings**
   - Click the Filtered YouTube icon in your Chrome toolbar. You’ll see options to:
     - **Hide recommended videos**
     - **Remove YouTube Shorts**
     - **Filter comments**
     - **Toggle the sidebar**
5. **Save and Test**
   - Close the settings and test your configuration. Adjust settings as needed for different use cases.

## FAQ: Your Top Questions About Filtered YouTube

### **1. Is Filtered YouTube free to use?**
Yes, the extension is completely free and open-source. You can install it directly from the Chrome Web Store without any subscription or payment.

### **2. Can I use it on mobile devices?**
Filtered YouTube works only on desktop browsers. For mobile, consider using YouTube’s **incognito mode** or third-party apps like **NewPipe** to minimize ads and recommendations.

### **3. Does Filtered YouTube interfere with YouTube Premium?**
No. The extension operates independently of your subscription status. You can use it with or without YouTube Premium.

Currently, Filtered YouTube hides entire sections (e.g., all recommended videos), not specific videos. For content filtering, use YouTube’s **block list** feature in settings.
