## Why Browser-Based Image Tools Outshine Cloud Alternatives

Before diving into the tools, let’s address the elephant in the room: Why choose browser-based image tools over cloud-based alternatives?

1. **Privacy First**: Your files stay local. No upload means no risk of data leaks or unwanted tracking.
2. **Speed**: Processing happens on your device, bypassing cloud queues. Even large files convert in seconds.
3. **No Accounts Required**: Skip the registration. Just drag and drop.
4. **Offline Compatibility**: Many browser tools work without an internet connection (depending on the tool).

These benefits make browser-based tools ideal for designers, developers, and anyone handling sensitive visuals.
