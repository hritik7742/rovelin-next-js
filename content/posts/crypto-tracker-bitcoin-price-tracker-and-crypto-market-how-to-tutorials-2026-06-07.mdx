## Step-by-Step: Installing and Activating Crypto Tracker

1. Visit the [Chrome Web Store page](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/gkpcjgecjgnliidfakfpmackhkklbdid) for Crypto Tracker
2. Click "Add to Chrome" and confirm the permissions request
3. Wait for the installation to complete (visible in the top-right browser corner)
4. Toggle the extension on via the puzzle piece icon in Chrome settings
